The Wilmette store

There’s a real place behind all this.

Cutco opened a store in Wilmette, and I spend time there. If you’d rather hold the knives than watch me hold them, say the word and we’ll go see them together.

What seeing them in person actually changes

  • You can feel the difference between a straight edge and a Double-D® before choosing.
  • Handle size stops being a guess — you hold it in your own hand.
  • You can compare two pieces side by side instead of from a photo.
  • Anything you already own can come with you for a sharpening hand-off.

Prefer not to travel? The demo works just as well over video — most of mine are.

Worth knowing

What the edges actually mean

The two edge types are the thing most people get wrong, and it changes what you should own. Straight from Cutco’s own placard on the store wall:

Straight edge
An ultra-sharp edge for smooth slicing, dicing and chopping, giving clean cuts.
Double-D® edge
Three recessed cutting edges that cut with less pressure, with points that shield the edges to keep them sharp.
Handle
Thermo-resin, which will not crack, chip, or absorb moisture — fitted for a large or small, left or right hand.

Source: Cutco “Anatomy of a Cutco Kitchen Knife” placard, photographed at the Wilmette store, July 2026. Anything about price or current specials belongs on cutco.com, not here.
