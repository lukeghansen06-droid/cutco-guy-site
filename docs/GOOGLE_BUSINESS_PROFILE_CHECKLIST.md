# Google Business Profile checklist

Use this only if the profile accurately represents an eligible business under Google's current rules. Do not create duplicate locations or use an address that customers cannot visit.

## Identity

- Business name matches the real-world name used with customers.
- Primary category accurately describes the service; avoid keyword stuffing.
- Phone: 312-659-4280.
- Website: https://cutcowithluke.com/
- Appointment URL: https://cutcowithluke.com/book
- Description is factual and does not imply Luke is Cutco corporate.

## Service area and address

- If customers do not visit a staffed storefront, configure a service-area business and hide the residential address.
- Add only areas Luke genuinely serves, such as the North Shore communities represented on the site.
- Keep hours aligned with real availability. Do not use 24/7 unless Luke truly responds 24/7.

## Trust assets

- Upload a clear Luke headshot and real, permission-cleared demo or product photos.
- Do not use customer faces without permission.
- Ask real customers for honest Google reviews; never offer incentives or provide review text for them to paste.
- Reply to every review calmly and specifically without exposing order details.

## Measurement

- Add UTM parameters to the GBP website link:
  `https://cutcowithluke.com/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`
- Add UTM parameters to the appointment link:
  `https://cutcowithluke.com/book?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp_booking`
- Confirm those sessions and `demo_booked` conversions appear in GA4 after the measurement ID is configured.

## Monthly upkeep

- Confirm phone, hours, booking link, and service area.
- Add one real photo or update when there is something genuine to show.
- Review search queries and booking conversions.
- Remove outdated offers immediately.
