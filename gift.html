The gift rule

For gifts, the goal is not the biggest thing. It’s something useful, easy to explain, and matched to the person’s kitchen — that’s what gets kept, used, and remembered.

Gift questions

How to choose without guessing

Do I need to know their exact set?

No. A photo of their drawer or block is enough for Luke to spot likely gaps and avoid duplicates.

Can I stay within a budget?

Yes. Share the occasion and range first. Luke can narrow the options, while current official pricing should always be confirmed on cutco.com.

What if they want something different?

Ask Luke about the current return or exchange terms before ordering. For larger gifts, involving the recipient is usually the safest move.
